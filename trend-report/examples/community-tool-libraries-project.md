## Scope

**In scope:**
- Tool libraries, "library of things" schemes, and community equipment shares in the UK and comparable countries
- Funding and governance models (council-run, volunteer-run, co-operative)
- Links to repair culture, right-to-repair movements, and reduced consumption

**Out of scope:**
- Commercial peer-to-peer rental platforms (unless directly informing civic models)
- Formal recycling and waste-disposal infrastructure

## Key Questions

1. What makes a community tool library financially sustainable beyond its first two years?
2. What role can local organisations play in seeding or supporting these schemes?
3. How does participation in a tool library change other household consumption habits, if at all?

## Primary Audience

Community organisers, local group coordinators, and programme staff — familiar with grassroots project work, avoid unnecessary jargon.

## Strategic Lens

| Pillar | Description | How this trend connects |

## Source Materials
