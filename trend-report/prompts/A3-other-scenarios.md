# Appendix 3: Other Scenarios

> **Appendix A3** | Target: ~500 words (±5%) | Pause for review after drafting

## Prompt

Draft **Appendix 3: Other Scenarios** (~500 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This appendix contains **Scenarios B and C** (the lead Scenario A is in the main body).

### Structure

Two scenario vignettes, each ~250 words:

#### Scenario B: [Title] — Transitional/Conflicted Future
- Set in a specific future year (e.g. 2028-2032)
- Name a protagonist with a specific role and location
- Show a "split-screen" reality: progress in some areas, persistent problems in others
- Include tension between automated/centralised and grassroots/volunteer responses
- End on a note of exhaustion or ambiguity — "holding the line" but not winning

#### Scenario C: [Title] — Cautionary/Pessimistic Future
- Set in a specific future year (e.g. 2030-2035)
- Name a protagonist who is personally affected by the trend going wrong
- Show systems failing: platforms not acting, resources depleted, trust eroded
- Include a moment where the protagonist hears/sees public opinion shaped by disinformation
- End on withdrawal/disengagement — not anger, but quieter defeat

### Tone Guidance

| Scenario | Dominant emotion | Ending |
|----------|-----------------|--------|
| B (Transitional) | Tension, vigilance | "Always playing catch-up" |
| C (Cautionary) | Disillusionment | Silence, withdrawal |

## Style Rules

- Write as a colleague, not a consultant. First person plural ("we") in any framing text around the vignettes.
- Present tense for vignettes
- Sensory details (weather, sounds, physical environment)
- Prioritise emotional directness over system description. Show how the change *feels*, not just how it works.
- Keep exposition minimal — if a policy or scheme needs explaining, do it through dialogue or action, not narration.
- At least one line of dialogue per scenario, kept natural and clipped (not speechy)
- UK settings preferred
- Show, don't tell
- Avoid consultant-speak in framing text: don't write "systemic failure", "institutional resilience", "governance deficit". Show what broke and how it feels. Before/after example: ❌ "Institutional resilience has deteriorated across the governance architecture" → ✅ "The council offices are half-empty. Nobody answers the phone any more."
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
