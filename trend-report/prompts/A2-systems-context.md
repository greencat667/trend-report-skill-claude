## Prompt

Draft **Appendix 2: Systems Context** (~400 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This section maps the system in which the trend operates.

### Structure

#### 0. Why we map the system (≤60 words)
Open with a brief paragraph explaining *why* systems mapping matters for strategic planning. Cover: what reinforcing loops are (cycles that amplify a trend) and balancing loops are (cycles that resist change); why identifying them helps us find leverage points — places where a small intervention can shift the whole system. Write in plain English for readers unfamiliar with systems thinking.

#### 1. Scope statement (≤50 words)
- What's included in this system analysis?
- What's explicitly out of scope?

#### 2. Key actors (≤150 words)
Identify actors across domains:
- **Institutions & regulators** — Government bodies, enforcement agencies
- **Private sector** — Platforms, tech providers
- **Civic groups & media** — NGOs, fact-checkers, journalists
- **International bodies** — Standards organisations, transnational networks
- **Communities** — Informal networks, volunteers

#### 3. Relationships & feedback loops (≤100 words)
- How do actors influence each other?
- What feedback loops exist (reinforcing or balancing)?
- How do economic incentives interact with public-interest aims?

#### 4. Tensions & collaborations (≤100 words)
Identify key tensions:
- Free expression vs harm prevention
- National regulation vs global platforms
- Commercial secrecy vs transparency
- Automated vs community-led moderation

Note where collaboration is evident (and its limits).

## Style Rules

- Write as a colleague, not a consultant. First person plural ("we", "our").
- Plain, direct English. Short sentences (target under 15 words). Break long sentences in two.
- Avoid consultant-speak: use plain language for systems concepts. Before/after example: ❌ "A reinforcing feedback loop between regulatory capture and market concentration drives systemic entrenchment" → ✅ "Weak regulation lets big players dominate. Dominant players then lobby for weaker regulation. The cycle tightens."
- Define technical terms briefly on first use — assume the reader is smart but not a specialist
- Systems thinking lens: actors, relationships, feedback, tensions
- Be specific about UK context
- Connect to SWOT analysis that follows
- **Citations:** Where you reference specific actors, policies, or data, cite inline as `([Source Name](https://url))`. Do not include a loose "Sources" list — every citation must be inline and linked.
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
