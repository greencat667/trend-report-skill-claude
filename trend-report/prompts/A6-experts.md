# Appendix 6: Key Experts

> **Appendix A6** | Target: ~200 words (±10%) | Draft near end — identifies people to talk to in Phase 2

## Prompt

Draft **Appendix 6: Key UK Experts & Actors** for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This is a reference list of **people and organisations to potentially consult in Phase 2** exploration — not necessarily people cited in the report. Use a broad definition of "expert" that goes well beyond academia.

Compile **10-15 experts and actors** relevant to the trend.

### Structure

A table with three columns:
1. **Name** — Full name (or organisation name for initiatives without a single figurehead)
2. **Job Title / Affiliation** — Current role and organisation
3. **Why Relevant** — 10-20 word explanation of their expertise and where to find their work

### Selection Criteria

Include a mix across **all** of these categories — do not let the list skew entirely toward academics and institutional figures:

- **Academics** — Researchers studying the trend (potential interviews, advisory input)
- **Practitioners & pioneer innovators** — People running relevant organisations or projects, especially those working at **community level** (potential partners)
- **Grassroots and community innovators** — Community organisers, local project leaders, social entrepreneurs working on the ground. These are often the people {{ORG}} would most want to learn from.
- **Civil society campaigners and movement leaders** — Campaigning groups, coalitions, and movement actors working on related issues. Include organisations {{ORG}} might ally with.
- **Policy experts** — Those influencing regulation or governance (potential allies or informants)
- **Institutional initiatives** — Toolkits or programmes that might inform {{ORG}}'s approach

Prioritise:
- UK-based or UK-focused experts and actors
- Publicly accessible work (publications, talks, toolkits, community projects)
- Diversity of perspectives, institutions, **and types of expertise** (not just published research)
- People and organisations {{ORG}} could realistically reach out to
- At least 3-4 entries should be grassroots/community/civil society actors, not academics

### Citation Format

End each "Why Relevant" cell with a linked citation to their work, e.g.:
- `([Full Fact](https://fullfact.org))` for org websites
- `([Cambridge Social Decision-Making Lab](https://...))` for research groups
- `([The Guardian](https://...))` for public writing

All citations must be parenthesized linked markdown. Do not include unlinked source names.

**No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
