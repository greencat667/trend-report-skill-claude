# Signals & Drivers (Summary)

> **Section 2** | Target: ~150 words (±5%) | Pause for review after drafting

## Prompt

Draft **Section 2: Top 5 Signals & Drivers** (~150 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This is the **summary version** for the main body. Full PESTEL detail goes in Appendix 1.

### Structure

1. **Opening sentence** (≤30 words) — A one-line scene-setter explaining why signals and drivers matter now.

2. **5 numbered signals/drivers** (≤25 words each) — Each should:
   - Have a bold heading (e.g. "1. UK regulatory enforcement")
   - Provide a crisp explanation of what's happening and why it matters
   - Note geography in parentheses where relevant

3. **Closing pointer** — Direct readers to the appendix for detailed PESTEL analysis with citations.

## Style Rules

- Write as a colleague, not a consultant. First person plural ("we", "our").
- Plain, direct English. Short sentences (target under 15 words).
- Numbered list format
- No inline citations in summary (they're in the appendix)
- Focus on *patterns*, not a shopping list
- Avoid consultant-speak: don't write "evolving landscape", "shifting paradigms", "key stakeholders". Name what's actually happening. Before/after example: ❌ "The evolving regulatory landscape is creating new compliance dynamics" → ✅ "New rules are coming in fast — and enforcement is getting real teeth."
- Strict markdown list formatting (blank line before/after list, one item per line)
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.

## Output Format

```markdown

[Opening sentence about why mapping signals/drivers matters]

1. **[Signal 1]** — [Explanation]

3. **[Signal 3]** — [Explanation]
4. **[Signal 4]** — [Explanation]
5. **[Signal 5]** — [Explanation]

*More detail on each signal & driver, including references, can be found in Appendix 1.*
```
