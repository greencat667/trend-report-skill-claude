# Appendix 4: Detailed SWOT Analysis

> **Appendix A4** | Target: ~400 words (±5%) | Pause for review after drafting

## Prompt

Draft **Appendix 4: Detailed SWOT Analysis** (~400 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This expands the summary grid in the main body into narrative explanations.

### Structure

Four sections, ~100 words each:

#### Strengths (internal capabilities)
- 3-4 strengths with narrative explanation
- Each strength: bold heading + 2-3 sentence explanation
- Focus on what {{ORG}} already has or does well, framed against the **strategic pillars** (from PROJECT.md)
- Include evidence where possible
- Where relevant, distinguish between strengths at a national/local level and at any international or parent-body level, if your organisation has both

#### Opportunities (external possibilities)
- 3-4 opportunities with narrative explanation
- Connect to signals/drivers from earlier analysis
- Show how {{ORG}} could exploit these

#### Weaknesses (internal gaps)
- 3-4 weaknesses with narrative explanation
- Be candid but constructive
- **Capacity is almost always the binding constraint** for a campaigning organisation. Ensure at least one weakness explicitly addresses capacity constraints (people, time, money, skills). Avoid weaknesses that could apply to any NGO — be specific to {{ORG}}'s actual situation.
- Also consider: skills gaps, institutional posture, risk appetite
- Where relevant, distinguish between weaknesses at a national/local level and at any international or parent-body level, if your organisation has both. If a weakness affects one but not the other, say so.

#### Threats (external risks)
- 3-4 threats with narrative explanation
- Connect to hostile actors, regulatory changes, platform volatility
- Include human impact (harassment, burnout)

### Style Rules

- Write as a colleague, not a consultant. First person plural ("we", "our").
- Plain, direct English. Short sentences (target under 15 words). Break long sentences in two.
- Avoid consultant-speak: don't write "strategic positioning", "leverage existing capabilities", "ecosystem alignment". Say what we can actually do and what's stopping us. Before/after example: ❌ "Our established credibility provides strategic positioning in multi-stakeholder forums" → ✅ "People trust us. When we show up to consultations, decision-makers listen — that's rare and worth protecting."
- Define technical terms briefly on first use — assume the reader is smart but not a specialist
- Bold the heading for each point
- Use en-dashes (–) not hyphens for punctuation
- Be specific to our organisation's context
- Balance honesty with constructive framing
- **Citations:** Where a strength, weakness, opportunity or threat references specific data or evidence, cite inline as `([Source Name](https://url))`. Do not include a loose "Sources" list — every citation must be inline and linked.
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
