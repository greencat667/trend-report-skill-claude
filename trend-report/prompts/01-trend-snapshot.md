# Trend Snapshot

> **Section 1** | Target: ~300 words (±5%) | Pause for review after drafting

## Prompt

Draft **Section 1: Trend Snapshot** (~300 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

Include three subsections:

### 1. Definition & scope (≤100 words)
- Define the core term(s) clearly and concisely
- Explain what "governance" means in this context (laws, platform rules, civic norms, technical standards)
- State what this trend focuses on (geographic scope, specific aspects)
- Briefly outline 3 broad approaches framing today's landscape

### 2. Why now (≤100 words)
- List 3 recent shifts that heighten urgency (use the same framing as the exec summary for consistency)
- Connect these to the broader climate (political, technological, social)
- End with a **relative urgency sentence**: "We assess this as a [watch-and-learn topic / emerging priority / live operational concern] for {{ORG}}." Calibrate honestly — resist defaulting to alarm. If the trend is important but not yet actionable, say so.

### 3. Relevance to {{ORG}} (≤100 words)
- Connect relevance to the organisation's **strategic pillars** (from PROJECT.md), not to a generic list of "core missions." Do not invent or assume what the organisation's missions are — use the pillars provided.
- For each pillar materially affected, state how in one sentence.
- Prioritise whichever pillar sits closest to the organisation's core theory of change — for a campaigning organisation this is often about grassroots organising, local groups, or alliance-building.
- What risks does inaction pose?
- Apply an equity lens: who is most affected and least reached?
- Where relevant, distinguish between implications at a national/local level and at any international or parent-body level, if your organisation has both. If the trend has different salience for each, say so briefly.

## Style Rules

- Write as a colleague, not a consultant. First person plural ("we", "our").
- Plain, direct English. Short sentences (target under 15 words). Break long sentences in two.
- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences)
- Use bullet points or numbered lists where they aid clarity
- Explain jargon on first use
- Avoid consultant-speak: don't write "multi-dimensional challenge", "governance landscape", "systemic approach". Say what the thing is and why it matters. Before/after example: ❌ "The governance landscape encompasses multiple regulatory dimensions" → ✅ "There are rules about this — some from government, some from platforms, some from industry. They don't always agree."
- Define technical terms briefly on first use — assume the reader is smart but not a specialist
- Include 1-2 linked citations for material claims: `([Source](https://...))`. All citations must be parenthesized linked markdown — no unlinked source names.
- Keep lists in strict markdown:
  - Add a blank line before each list
  - Keep one item per line
  - Do not place list items on the same line as the intro sentence
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
