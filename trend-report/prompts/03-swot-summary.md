# SWOT Analysis (Summary)

> **Section 3** | Target: ~150 words (±5%) | Pause for review after drafting

## Prompt

Draft **Section 3: SWOT Analysis** (~150 words) for the report on **{{TOPIC}}**.

This is the **summary grid** for the main body. Detailed narrative goes in Appendix 4.

### Structure

1. **Title line** with subtitle: "A current assessment of our organisation"

2. **2×2 grid** with 3-4 bullet points per quadrant:
   - **Strengths** — Internal capabilities and assets
   - **Weaknesses** — Internal gaps and limitations
   - **Opportunities** — External possibilities to exploit
   - **Threats** — External risks and challenges

3. **Closing pointer** — Direct readers to the appendix for detailed SWOT narrative.

## Style Rules

- Bullet points only (no paragraphs)
- Each bullet: 3-8 words, sentence case
- No citations in summary
- Balance across quadrants (similar number of points)
- Weaknesses must include at least one point about **capacity** (people, time, money, skills) — this is typically the binding constraint for a campaigning organisation
- Frame strengths and weaknesses relative to the organisation's **strategic pillars** (from PROJECT.md) where possible
- Avoid consultant-speak in bullet text: use plain, specific language. Before/after: ❌ "Strategic network positioning" → ✅ "Strong alliances with local groups"
- **No meta-text:** Do not include word counts, drafting notes, or other artefacts in your output.
